# Hot pot sauces: nuts, peanuts, sesame, fish

Example research for a guest who avoids tree nuts, peanuts and fish (shellfish OK). Not a safety guarantee. Read the jar in your hand: labels change between versions and countries.

## Labels not in English

- Imported food sold in the US must be labeled in English ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/industry/importing-fda-regulated-products/importing-human-foods)). If a label uses another language too, all required statements must appear in both ([FDA CPG 562.400](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/cpg-sec-562400-foreign-language-declarations-food-labels)).
- The English sticker can be wrong or incomplete. If the sticker and the original label disagree, skip it.
- Korea's required allergen list covers peanut, walnut and pine nut, but not almond or cashew ([MFDS](https://mfds.go.kr/eng/wpge/m_14/denofile.do)).
- Japan requires peanut, walnut and cashew (cashew since April 2026), but sesame and almond are only "recommended" ([CAA](https://www.caa.go.jp/en/policy/food_labeling/assets/food_labeling_cms204_240425_01.pdf)).
